## Uploading to itch.io

[itch.io](https://itch.io/)
[Your first itch.io page](https://itch.io/docs/creators/getting-started)

1. Create a zipped folder of your Windows build. Make sure to zip the entire folder of your build
2. Register an [itch.io](https://itch.io/) account
3. Navigate to your Dashboard
4. Select Create a New Project
5. Your itch.io page should include:
	- A zipped Windows build of your project
	- 3 screen shots of your project
	- The title of your project
	- A short paragraph description with controls information
6. Set page to Public and Press Save at the top or bottom of your Edit page to share your game.
