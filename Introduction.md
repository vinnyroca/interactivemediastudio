## What is Interactive Media Art

### New Media Art

Art made with the "new" media a.k.a media that is shared online and through computers.

Historically referred to with a variety of titles such as computer art, digital art, or electronic art.

### Media Art

Art with technology

### Interactive Media Art

Art with technology that is interactive

Encompasses a variety of types of projects:

- Games 
- Interactive Installations
- Applications
- Websites
- Further
