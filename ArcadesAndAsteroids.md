## Spacewar!

- Commonly understood as first, intentionally designed video game.
- Developed at MIT on the PDP-1 in 1961
- Code was "open source" and played at many universities

## Characteristics of Arcade Games

- Designed to attract the public
- Designed to be easy to understand to invite players
- Repetitive game loop
- Designed to be difficult to advance to extract money
- Designed to be competitive with high scores
- Historically, designed specifically for a young, white, male audience with disposable income
