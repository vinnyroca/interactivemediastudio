# Arcades and Asteroids


The use of Asteroids as the base project for this unit is inspired by [Paolo Pedercini](https://paolo.molleindustria.org/) and his Experimental Game Design Course

Early emulation of Existing Games
Spacewar - MIT hacker club, open source shared among many computers
Mutacope
Pinball Arcades
Lens Men
Computer Space
Pong
Asteroids

Characteristics of Arcade Games

Designed to Attract the public
Designed to be easy to immediately understand to invite players
Designed to be difficult to advance to extract quarter
Designed to be competitive with high scores
Historically, designed specifically for a young, white, male audience with disposable income
